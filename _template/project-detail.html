Project Overview

FinTech Corp approached me to overhaul their existing customer dashboard. The original platform suffered from information overload, causing users to feel overwhelmed when checking their investment portfolios.

The goal was to create a streamlined, cleaner interface that surfaced the most critical data first, allowing users to drill down into details only when necessary. We aimed to modernize the visual language while improving accessibility standards across the application.

warning

The Challenge

Users were bouncing from the dashboard within 15 seconds. Heatmaps showed that the navigation was confusing, and the sheer volume of numbers on the screen created cognitive load. The challenge was to reduce complexity without removing functionality for power users.

The Process

1

Research & Discovery

I conducted 15 user interviews to understand pain points. We discovered that 80% of users only cared about 3 specific metrics: Total Balance, Recent Activity, and Monthly Spending.

2

Wireframing & Prototyping

We created low-fidelity wireframes to test the new information architecture. The focus was on a "card-based" layout that grouped related information together.

3

Final UI Design

The final UI adopted a dark-themed aesthetic to reduce eye strain for night-time traders. We used vibrant gradients for positive trends to make success feel rewarding.

The Solution

The redesigned dashboard introduced a customizable widget system. Users can now drag and drop the metrics they care about most to the top of the page. We also introduced "Smart Insights," a feature that uses AI to summarize weekly spending in plain English.
